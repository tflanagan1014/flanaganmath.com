Lesson 43: Parametric Surfaces

A curve is described by a vector function of one parameter, \(\mathbf{r}(t)\). A surface is described by a vector function of two parameters, \(u\) and \(v\).

Parametric Surface

\[ \mathbf{R}(u, v) = \langle x(u, v),\ y(u, v),\ z(u, v) \rangle \]

An Example

Show that every point of the surface below lies on the plane \(x + y + z = 10\).

\[ \mathbf{R}(u, v) = \langle u,\ v,\ 10 - u - v \rangle \]

Here \(x = u\), \(y = v\), and \(z = 10 - u - v\).

\[ \begin{aligned} x + y + z &= u + v + (10 - u - v) \\ &= 10 \end{aligned} \]

An Example

Show that every point of the surface below lies on the sphere \(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4\).

\[ \begin{aligned} &\mathbf{R}(u, v) = \langle 2\sin v\cos u,\ 2\sin v\sin u,\ 2\cos v \rangle, \\ &\qquad 0 \le u \le 2\pi,\quad 0 \le v \le \pi \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} &x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \\ &\quad = 4\sin^2 v\cos^2 u + 4\sin^2 v\sin^2 u + 4\cos^2 v \\ &\quad = 4\sin^2 v\left(\cos^2 u + \sin^2 u\right) + 4\cos^2 v \\ &\quad = 4\sin^2 v + 4\cos^2 v \\ &\quad = 4 \end{aligned} \]
