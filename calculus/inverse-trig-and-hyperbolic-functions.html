Lesson 19: Derivatives of Inverse Trig and Hyperbolic Functions

Derivatives of the Inverse Trigonometric Functions

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{d}{dx}\left[\sin^{-1} x\right] &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} \\ \frac{d}{dx}\left[\cos^{-1} x\right] &= -\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} \\ \frac{d}{dx}\left[\tan^{-1} x\right] &= \frac{1}{1 + x^2} \\ \frac{d}{dx}\left[\sec^{-1} x\right] &= \frac{1}{|x|\sqrt{x^2 - 1}} \end{aligned} \]

Show that \(\dfrac{d}{dx}\left[\tan^{-1} x\right] = \dfrac{1}{1 + x^2}\).

Let \(y = \tan^{-1} x\), so \(\tan y = x\). Differentiate implicitly.

\[ \begin{aligned} \sec^2 y\,\frac{dy}{dx} &= 1 \\ \frac{dy}{dx} &= \frac{1}{\sec^2 y} \\ &= \frac{1}{1 + \tan^2 y} \\ &= \frac{1}{1 + x^2} \end{aligned} \]

Differentiate \(f(x) = \sin^{-1}(4x + 1)\).

\[ f'(x) = \frac{4}{\sqrt{1 - (4x + 1)^2}} \]

Differentiate \(f(x) = \sin^{-1}(3x)\).

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \frac{3}{\sqrt{1 - (3x)^2}} \\ &= \frac{3}{\sqrt{1 - 9x^2}} \end{aligned} \]

Hyperbolic Sine and Cosine

\[ \begin{aligned} &\sinh x = \frac{e^x - e^{-x}}{2} \\ &\cosh x = \frac{e^x + e^{-x}}{2} \end{aligned} \]

Their Derivatives

\[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{d}{dx}[\sinh x] = \cosh x \\ &\frac{d}{dx}[\cosh x] = \sinh x \end{aligned} \]

Show that \(\dfrac{d}{dx}[\sinh x] = \cosh x\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{d}{dx}[\sinh x] &= \frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{e^x - e^{-x}}{2}\right] \\ &= \frac{e^x - \left(-e^{-x}\right)}{2} \\ &= \frac{e^x + e^{-x}}{2} \\ &= \cosh x \end{aligned} \]
