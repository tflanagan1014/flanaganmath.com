Lesson 4: Finding Limits Algebraically

If plugging in gives a number, that number is the limit.

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x + 8}{2x + 1}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x + 8}{2x + 1} &= \frac{0 + 8}{2(0) + 1} \\ &= 8 \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(x + e)}{\tan^{-1} x + 1}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(x + e)}{\tan^{-1} x + 1} &= \frac{\ln(0 + e)}{\tan^{-1} 0 + 1} \\ &= \frac{1}{0 + 1} \\ &= 1 \end{aligned} \]

If plugging in gives \(\tfrac{0}{0}\), try to factor and cancel.

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{x - 4}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{x - 4} &= \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{(x - 4)(x - 3)}{x - 4} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 4} (x - 3) \\ &= 4 - 3 \\ &= 1 \end{aligned} \]

This matches what we found with a table of values.

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 2} \frac{3x^2 - 10x + 8}{x - 2}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{3x^2 - 10x + 8}{x - 2} &= \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{(x - 2)(3x - 4)}{x - 2} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 2} (3x - 4) \\ &= 3(2) - 4 \\ &= 2 \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{4 + x} - 2}{x}\) without a calculator.

Multiply the top and bottom by the conjugate of the numerator.

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{4 + x} - 2}{x} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{4 + x} - 2}{x} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{4 + x} + 2}{\sqrt{4 + x} + 2} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(4 + x) - 4}{x\left(\sqrt{4 + x} + 2\right)} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{x\left(\sqrt{4 + x} + 2\right)} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{\sqrt{4 + x} + 2} \\ &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4} + 2} \\ &= \frac{1}{4} \end{aligned} \]

Three Important Limits

\[ \begin{aligned} &\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{x} = 1 \\ &\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1 \\ &\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \cos x}{x} = 0 \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{8x + 1 - \cos x}{x}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{8x + 1 - \cos x}{x} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \left(8 + \frac{1 - \cos x}{x}\right) \\ &= 8 + 0 \\ &= 8 \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1 + 7x - \sin x}{x}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1 + 7x - \sin x}{x} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \left(\frac{e^x - 1}{x} + 7 - \frac{\sin x}{x}\right) \\ &= 1 + 7 - 1 \\ &= 7 \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{3\sin(4x)}{\sin(7x)}\).

Multiply and divide to create \(\frac{\sin(4x)}{4x}\) and \(\frac{\sin(7x)}{7x}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{3\sin(4x)}{\sin(7x)} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{3 \cdot 4x \cdot \dfrac{\sin(4x)}{4x}}{7x \cdot \dfrac{\sin(7x)}{7x}} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{12}{7} \cdot \frac{\dfrac{\sin(4x)}{4x}}{\dfrac{\sin(7x)}{7x}} \\ &= \frac{12}{7} \cdot \frac{1}{1} \\ &= \frac{12}{7} \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{\omega \to 0} \frac{\tan\omega + \sec\omega - 1}{\omega\sec\omega}\).

Split the fraction. Use \(\frac{\tan\omega}{\sec\omega} = \sin\omega\) and \(\frac{\sec\omega - 1}{\sec\omega} = 1 - \cos\omega\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{\omega \to 0} \frac{\tan\omega + \sec\omega - 1}{\omega\sec\omega} &= \lim_{\omega \to 0} \left(\frac{\tan\omega}{\omega\sec\omega} + \frac{\sec\omega - 1}{\omega\sec\omega}\right) \\ &= \lim_{\omega \to 0} \left(\frac{\sin\omega}{\omega} + \frac{1 - \cos\omega}{\omega}\right) \\ &= 1 + 0 \\ &= 1 \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^{2x} - 1}{x\left(e^x + 1\right)}\).

Factor the numerator as a difference of squares: \(e^{2x} - 1 = \left(e^x - 1\right)\left(e^x + 1\right)\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^{2x} - 1}{x\left(e^x + 1\right)} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\left(e^x - 1\right)\left(e^x + 1\right)}{x\left(e^x + 1\right)} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{x} \\ &= 1 \end{aligned} \]

Assume \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to c} f(x) = 5\) and \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to c} g(x) = 2\). Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to c} \left[4f(x) + g(x)\right]\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to c} \left[4f(x) + g(x)\right] &= \lim_{x \to c} 4f(x) + \lim_{x \to c} g(x) \\ &= 4\lim_{x \to c} f(x) + \lim_{x \to c} g(x) \\ &= 4(5) + 2 \\ &= 22 \end{aligned} \]
