Lesson 35: U-Substitution

U-substitution undoes the chain rule. Choose \(u\) to be an inside function whose derivative also appears in the integral. Then rewrite everything in terms of \(u\).

The largest part of our final exam is evaluating integrals with u-substitution. This skill is also essential on day one of Calculus 2.

Find \(\displaystyle\int 8x\left(6x^2 + 167\right)^{17}dx\).

\[ \begin{aligned} u &= 6x^2 + 167 \\ du &= 12x\,dx \\ 8x\,dx &= \frac{2}{3}\,du \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \int 8x\left(6x^2 + 167\right)^{17}dx &= \frac{2}{3}\int u^{17}\,du \\ &= \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{u^{18}}{18} + C \\ &= \frac{\left(6x^2 + 167\right)^{18}}{27} + C \end{aligned} \]

Find \(\displaystyle\int 10\sqrt[4]{2x + 83}\,dx\).

\[ \begin{aligned} u &= 2x + 83 \\ du &= 2\,dx \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \int 10\sqrt[4]{2x + 83}\,dx &= 5\int u^{\frac{1}{4}}\,du \\ &= 5 \cdot \frac{4}{5}u^{\frac{5}{4}} + C \\ &= 4(2x + 83)^{\frac{5}{4}} + C \end{aligned} \]

Find \(\displaystyle\int 6e^{12x}\left(7e^{12x} + 72\right)^7 dx\).

\[ \begin{aligned} u &= 7e^{12x} + 72 \\ du &= 84e^{12x}\,dx \\ 6e^{12x}\,dx &= \frac{1}{14}\,du \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \int 6e^{12x}\left(7e^{12x} + 72\right)^7 dx &= \frac{1}{14}\int u^7\,du \\ &= \frac{u^8}{112} + C \\ &= \frac{\left(7e^{12x} + 72\right)^8}{112} + C \end{aligned} \]

Find \(\displaystyle\int 17x^6\sin\left(20x^7 + 27\right)dx\).

\[ \begin{aligned} u &= 20x^7 + 27 \\ du &= 140x^6\,dx \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \int 17x^6\sin\left(20x^7 + 27\right)dx &= \frac{17}{140}\int \sin u\,du \\ &= -\frac{17}{140}\cos\left(20x^7 + 27\right) + C \end{aligned} \]

Find \(\displaystyle 3\int \sec^2\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\theta\right)d\theta\).

\[ \begin{aligned} u &= \frac{\pi}{4}\theta \\ du &= \frac{\pi}{4}\,d\theta \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} 3\int \sec^2\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\theta\right)d\theta &= \frac{12}{\pi}\int \sec^2 u\,du \\ &= \frac{12}{\pi}\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\theta\right) + C \end{aligned} \]

Find \(\displaystyle\int \frac{-2x^3}{10x^4 + 31}\,dx\).

\[ \begin{aligned} u &= 10x^4 + 31 \\ du &= 40x^3\,dx \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \int \frac{-2x^3}{10x^4 + 31}\,dx &= -\frac{1}{20}\int \frac{1}{u}\,du \\ &= -\frac{1}{20}\ln\left(10x^4 + 31\right) + C \end{aligned} \]

Find \(\displaystyle\int \frac{9}{\sqrt{81 - 16x^2}}\,dx\).

Factor out 81 to match \(\displaystyle\int \frac{du}{\sqrt{1 - u^2}} = \sin^{-1} u + C\).

\[ \int \frac{9}{\sqrt{81 - 16x^2}}\,dx = \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{4x}{9}\right)^2}}\,dx \]
\[ \begin{aligned} u &= \frac{4x}{9} \\ du &= \frac{4}{9}\,dx \end{aligned} \]
\[ \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - u^2}} \cdot \frac{9}{4}\,du = \frac{9}{4}\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{4x}{9}\right) + C \]

Change the limits of integration to u-values, and you never have to go back to x.

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_0^1 x^4\left(x^5 - 2\right)^7 dx\).

\[ \begin{aligned} u &= x^5 - 2 \\ du &= 5x^4\,dx \end{aligned} \]

When \(x = 0\), \(u = -2\). When \(x = 1\), \(u = -1\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \int_0^1 x^4\left(x^5 - 2\right)^7 dx &= \frac{1}{5}\int_{-2}^{-1} u^7\,du \\ &= \frac{1}{5}\left[\frac{u^8}{8}\right]_{-2}^{-1} \\ &= \frac{1}{40}(1 - 256) \\ &= -\frac{51}{8} \end{aligned} \]
