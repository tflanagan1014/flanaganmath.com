Lesson 40: The Washer Method (y-Axis)

Rotate the region between \(x = f(y)\) and \(x = g(y)\), with \(f \ge g\), about the \(y\)-axis.

The Washer Method (about the y-axis)

\[ V = \int_c^d \pi\left(\left[f(y)\right]^2 - \left[g(y)\right]^2\right)dy \]

Find the volume of the solid obtained by revolving the region between \(x = 9y\) and \(x = y^2\) about the \(y\)-axis.

\[ \begin{aligned} 9y &= y^2 \\ y(y - 9) &= 0 \end{aligned} \]

So the curves meet at \(y = 0\) and \(y = 9\). On \([0, 9]\), \(9y \ge y^2\), so the outer radius is \(9y\).

\[ \begin{aligned} V &= \int_0^9 \pi\left(81y^2 - y^4\right)dy \\ &= \pi\left[27y^3 - \frac{y^5}{5}\right]_0^9 \\ &= \pi\left(19{,}683 - \frac{59{,}049}{5}\right) \\ &= \frac{39{,}366\pi}{5} \end{aligned} \]
