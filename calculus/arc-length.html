Lesson 42: Arc Length

Arc Length

\[ L = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + \left[f\,'(x)\right]^2}\,dx \]

Imagine an object moving along the graph from \((a, f(a))\) to \((b, f(b))\). The arc length is the “mileage” of that motion: the actual distance traveled along the curve.

Find the arc length of the graph of \(f(x) = \ln|\sec x|\) from \(x = 0\) to \(x = \frac{\pi}{6}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \frac{\sec x\tan x}{\sec x} \\ &= \tan x \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \sqrt{1 + \tan^2 x} &= \sqrt{\sec^2 x} \\ &= \sec x \end{aligned} \]

Use \(\displaystyle\int \sec x\,dx = \ln|\sec x + \tan x| + C\).

\[ \begin{aligned} L &= \int_0^{\pi/6} \sec x\,dx \\ &= \Big[\ln|\sec x + \tan x|\Big]_0^{\pi/6} \\ &= \ln\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right) - \ln 1 \\ &= \ln\sqrt{3} \\ &= \frac{1}{2}\ln 3 \end{aligned} \]
