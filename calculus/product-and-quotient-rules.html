Lesson 16: The Product and Quotient Rules

The Product Rule

\[ \frac{d}{dx}[f(x)\,g(x)] = f'(x)\,g(x) + f(x)\,g'(x) \]

Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for \(y = x^2 \sin x\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{dy}{dx} &= (2x)(\sin x) + \left(x^2\right)(\cos x) \\ &= 2x\sin x + x^2\cos x \end{aligned} \]

Prove the product rule.

Subtract and add \(f(x + h)\,g(x)\) in the numerator.

\[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{d}{dx}[f(x)\,g(x)] \\ &\quad = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h)g(x + h) - f(x)g(x)}{h} \\ &\quad = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + h)g(x + h) - f(x + h)g(x)}{h} \\ &\qquad\quad {}+ \frac{f(x + h)g(x) - f(x)g(x)}{h} \\ &\quad = \lim_{h \to 0} f(x + h)\,\frac{g(x + h) - g(x)}{h} \\ &\qquad\quad {}+ g(x)\,\frac{f(x + h) - f(x)}{h} \\ &\quad = f(x)\,g'(x) + g(x)\,f'(x) \end{aligned} \]

Here \(f(x + h) \to f(x)\) because a differentiable function is continuous.

A Sample Exam Problem

Differentiate \(f(\theta) = 4 + 3\cos\theta + 7\theta\sin\theta\).

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(\theta) &= -3\sin\theta + 7\sin\theta + 7\theta\cos\theta \\ &= 4\sin\theta + 7\theta\cos\theta \end{aligned} \]

The Quotient Rule

\[ \frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right] = \frac{f'(x)\,g(x) - f(x)\,g'(x)}{[g(x)]^2} \]

Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for \(y = \dfrac{8x}{3x + 7}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{dy}{dx} &= \frac{(8)(3x + 7) - (8x)(3)}{(3x + 7)^2} \\ &= \frac{24x + 56 - 24x}{(3x + 7)^2} \\ &= \frac{56}{(3x + 7)^2} \end{aligned} \]

Use the quotient rule to show \(\dfrac{d}{dx}[\tan x] = \sec^2 x\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{d}{dx}[\tan x] &= \frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}\right] \\ &= \frac{(\cos x)(\cos x) - (\sin x)(-\sin x)}{\cos^2 x} \\ &= \frac{\cos^2 x + \sin^2 x}{\cos^2 x} \\ &= \frac{1}{\cos^2 x} \\ &= \sec^2 x \end{aligned} \]

A Sample Exam Problem

Differentiate \(f(x) = \dfrac{2x - 7}{5x - 9}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \frac{(2)(5x - 9) - (2x - 7)(5)}{(5x - 9)^2} \\ &= \frac{10x - 18 - 10x + 35}{(5x - 9)^2} \\ &= \frac{17}{(5x - 9)^2} \end{aligned} \]
