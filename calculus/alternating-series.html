Lesson 60: Alternating Series

An alternating series has terms that switch sign.

The Alternating Series Test

\[ \begin{aligned} &\text{If } |a_n| \text{ is decreasing and } \lim_{n \to \infty} |a_n| = 0, \\ &\text{the alternating series converges.} \end{aligned} \]

Otherwise, the test is inconclusive.

Does the alternating harmonic series \(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{k+1}}{k}\) converge?

The terms \(\frac{1}{k}\) are decreasing and approach 0, so the series converges, even though the harmonic series \(\sum \frac{1}{k}\) diverges!
