Help with Homework #17 – Dimensional Analysis

Problem 1

The mass of an object is increasing at a rate of 54 grams per minute. What is this rate in kilograms per hour?

Round your answer to the nearest tenth.

54 g1 min×60 min1 hr×1 kg1000 g=3.24 kg1 hr
≈3.2 kg per hr

Conversions used: 1 hr = 60 min   |   1 kg = 1000 g
