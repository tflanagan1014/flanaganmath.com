Help with Homework #18 – Reasoning with the LCM or the GCD

Problem 1

Find the least common multiple of 12 and 15.

Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48, 60

Multiples of 15: 15, 30, 45, 60

The LCM of 12 and 15 is 60.

Problem 2

Two employees take breaks at different intervals. Employee A takes a break every 4 hours, and Employee B takes a break every 6 hours. If both employees take a break at 9:00 AM, when will they take their next break together?

Employee A (every 4 hours): 9:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 5:00 PM, 9:00 PM

Employee B (every 6 hours): 9:00 AM, 3:00 PM, 9:00 PM

They take their next break together at 9:00 PM.

Problem 3

A bus leaves the station every 3 hours. Another bus leaves every 5 hours. Both buses depart at 7:00 AM. When will the two buses depart together again?

First bus (every 3 hours): 7:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 4:00 PM, 7:00 PM, 10:00 PM

Second bus (every 5 hours): 7:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 5:00 PM, 10:00 PM

The two buses depart together again at 10:00 PM.

Problem 4

Find the greatest common divisor of 48 and 36.

Ways to multiply to get 36

1362183124966

Ways to multiply to get 48

14822431641268

Divisors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36

Divisors of 48: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48

Common divisors: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

The GCD of 48 and 36 is 12.

Problem 5

A hospital has 32 masks and 56 pairs of gloves. They want to make identical kits with the same number of masks and pairs of gloves in each kit.

a) What is the greatest number of kits that can be made with no leftovers?

Ways to multiply to get 32

13221648

Ways to multiply to get 56

15622841478

Divisors of 32: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32

Divisors of 56: 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 14, 28, 56

Common divisors: 1, 2, 4, 8

The greatest number of kits is 8.

b) How many masks are in each kit?

32 ÷ 8 = 4

There are 4 masks in each kit.

c) How many pairs of gloves are in each kit?

56 ÷ 8 = 7

There are 7 pairs of gloves in each kit.
