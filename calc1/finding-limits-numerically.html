\[ f(x) = \frac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{x - 4} \]

The function is undefined when \(x = 4\). But as \(x\) approaches 4, we see a pattern: \(f(x)\) approaches 1.

This is the idea of a limit. We write it as below.

\[ \lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{x - 4} = 1 \]

From the left, \(f(x)\) approaches 2. This is the left-hand limit. From the right, \(f(x)\) approaches 2. This is the right-hand limit.

\[ \lim_{x \to 1^-} f(x) = 2 \qquad \lim_{x \to 1^+} f(x) = 2 \]

Idea: When the left-hand and right-hand limits agree, the two-sided limit exists and equals that number.

\[ \lim_{x \to 1} f(x) = 2 \]

The left-hand and right-hand limits are different, so \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} f(x)\) does not exist.

When the left-hand and right-hand limits are different, we say the limit does not exist.

Three Important Limits

\[ \begin{aligned} &\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{x} = 1 \\ &\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1 \\ &\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \cos x}{x} = 0 \end{aligned} \]

The letter we use for the variable does not matter.
