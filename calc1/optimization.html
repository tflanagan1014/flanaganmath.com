Lesson 17: Optimization

In an optimization problem, we find the largest or smallest value of a quantity. Write a function for the quantity in one variable, find its critical numbers, and check that you have a maximum or minimum.

A rectangle must have a perimeter of 236 ft. Find the maximum area it can have.

\[ \begin{aligned} 2x + 2y &= 236 \\ y &= 118 - x \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} A(x) &= x(118 - x) = 118x - x^2 \\ A'(x) &= 118 - 2x = 0 \\ x &= 59 \end{aligned} \]

\(A\,''(x) = -2 < 0\), so this is a maximum. The rectangle is a 59 by 59 square, with area \(3481\) square feet.

A cylinder with volume 46 cubic centimeters is to be made. Find the radius that minimizes its surface area, to the nearest hundredth.

\[ \begin{aligned} \pi r^2 h &= 46 \\ h &= \frac{46}{\pi r^2} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} S(r) &= 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h \\ &= 2\pi r^2 + \frac{92}{r} \\ S'(r) &= 4\pi r - \frac{92}{r^2} = 0 \\ r^3 &= \frac{23}{\pi} \\ r &\approx 1.94 \end{aligned} \]

The radius is about \(1.94\) centimeters.

A star is at the point \((268, 0)\). A spacecraft travels along the path \(y = \sqrt{x}\). Find the distance of closest approach, to the nearest hundredth.

Minimizing the distance is the same as minimizing its square.

\[ \begin{aligned} D^2 &= (x - 268)^2 + \left(\sqrt{x}\right)^2 \\ &= (x - 268)^2 + x \\ \frac{d}{dx}\left[D^2\right] &= 2(x - 268) + 1 = 0 \\ x &= 267.5 \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} D &= \sqrt{(-0.5)^2 + 267.5} \\ &= \sqrt{267.75} \\ &\approx 16.36 \end{aligned} \]

A closed box with a square base must have a volume of 2025 cubic feet. The top and bottom cost \$5 per square foot, and the sides cost \$225 per square foot. Find the minimum cost of materials.

Let the base be \(x\) by \(x\) and the height \(h\).

\[ \begin{aligned} x^2 h &= 2025 \\ h &= \frac{2025}{x^2} \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} C(x) &= 5\left(2x^2\right) + 225(4xh) \\ &= 10x^2 + \frac{1{,}822{,}500}{x} \\ C'(x) &= 20x - \frac{1{,}822{,}500}{x^2} = 0 \\ x^3 &= 91{,}125 \\ x &= 45 \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} C(45) &= 20{,}250 + 40{,}500 \\ &= 60{,}750 \end{aligned} \]

The minimum cost is \$60,750.

A Sample Exam Problem

A closed box with a square base must have a volume of 324 cubic feet. The top and bottom cost \$9 per square foot, and the sides cost \$162 per square foot. Find the minimum cost of materials.

\[ \begin{aligned} h &= \frac{324}{x^2} \\ C(x) &= 9\left(2x^2\right) + 162(4xh) \\ &= 18x^2 + \frac{209{,}952}{x} \\ C'(x) &= 36x - \frac{209{,}952}{x^2} = 0 \\ x^3 &= 5832 \\ x &= 18 \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} C(18) &= 5832 + 11{,}664 \\ &= 17{,}496 \end{aligned} \]

The minimum cost is \$17,496.
