Lesson 18: L'Hôpital's Rule

Suppose \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\) are differentiable, and plugging in \(x = c\) gives \(\tfrac{0}{0}\) or \(\tfrac{\pm\infty}{\pm\infty}\). Then the rule below applies.

L’Hôpital’s Rule

\[ \lim_{x \to c} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim_{x \to c} \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)} \]

This is incredibly useful, as you will see!

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x - 1}{x^7 - 1}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x - 1}{x^7 - 1} &= \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{1}{7x^6} \\ &= \frac{1}{7} \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{17x + 1}{19x + 9}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{17x + 1}{19x + 9} &= \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{17}{19} \\ &= \frac{17}{19} \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1 - x}{31x^2}\). The rule may be used more than once.

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1 - x}{31x^2} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{62x} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x}{62} \\ &= \frac{1}{62} \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(15\pi x)}{240x}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(15\pi x)}{240x} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{15\pi\cos(15\pi x)}{240} \\ &= \frac{15\pi}{240} \\ &= \frac{\pi}{16} \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 1} \left(\frac{1}{\ln x} - \frac{x}{\ln x}\right)\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{1 - x}{\ln x} &= \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{-1}{\frac{1}{x}} \\ &= -1 \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0^+} x\cot(4\pi x)\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{x}{\tan(4\pi x)} &= \lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{1}{4\pi\sec^2(4\pi x)} \\ &= \frac{1}{4\pi} \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} (1 + 2x)^{1/x}\) using logarithms.

Let \(y = (1 + 2x)^{1/x}\). Then \(\ln y = \dfrac{\ln(1 + 2x)}{x}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(1 + 2x)}{x} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\frac{2}{1 + 2x}}{1} \\ &= 2 \end{aligned} \]

Since \(\ln y \to 2\), we get \(y \to e^2\).

\[ \lim_{x \to 0} (1 + 2x)^{1/x} = e^2 \]

Sample Exam Problems

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to -6} \frac{x + 6}{x^5 + 7776}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to -6} \frac{x + 6}{x^5 + 7776} &= \lim_{x \to -6} \frac{1}{5x^4} \\ &= \frac{1}{5(1296)} \\ &= \frac{1}{6480} \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{6x^4 - 17x^2}{5x^3 + 11x^2}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{6x^4 - 17x^2}{5x^3 + 11x^2} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{24x^3 - 34x}{15x^2 + 22x} \\ &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{72x^2 - 34}{30x + 22} \\ &= -\frac{34}{22} \\ &= -\frac{17}{11} \end{aligned} \]
