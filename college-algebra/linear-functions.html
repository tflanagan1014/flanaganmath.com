Module 4 – Topic 6

Linear Functions

A linear function has the form \(f(x)=mx+b\). Its graph is a line.

The slope \(m=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\) is the rate of change: how much \(y\) changes when \(x\) goes up by \(1\). The number \(b\) is the \(y\)-intercept, the value when \(x=0\).

Slope

\[ m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1} \]

In applications, the slope is a per-unit rate (dollars per mile, cost per item) and \(b\) is a fixed starting amount.

Example 1

Find the linear function through \((1,\,4)\) and \((3,\,10)\).

Find the slope.

\[ m=\frac{10-4}{3-1}=\frac{6}{2}=3 \]

Put \(m=3\) and the point \((1,\,4)\) into \(y=mx+b\) to find \(b\).

\[ \begin{aligned} 4 &= 3(1)+b \\ 1 &= b \end{aligned} \]
\[ f(x)=3x+1 \]

Example 2

A truck rental costs \(\$30\) plus \(\$1.50\) per mile.

  1. Write the cost function \(C(x)\).
  2. Find \(C(120)\).
  3. How many miles can you drive for \(\$96\)?

a. The per-mile rate is the slope, and the fixed \(\$30\) is \(b\). Let \(x\) be the number of miles.

\[ C(x)=1.50x+30 \]

b.

\[ \begin{aligned} C(120) &= 1.50(120)+30 \\ &= 180+30 \\ &= 210 \end{aligned} \]

Driving \(120\) miles costs \(\$210\).

c. Set \(C(x)=96\) and solve.

\[ \begin{aligned} 1.50x+30 &= 96 \\ 1.50x &= 66 \\ x &= 44 \end{aligned} \]

You can drive \(44\) miles for \(\$96\).

Cost is the fixed cost plus the cost per unit times the number of units. Revenue is the price times the number of units sold. Profit is what is left: \(P(x)=R(x)-C(x)\).

Profit

\[ P(x)=R(x)-C(x) \]

The break-even point is where there is no profit and no loss: set \(R(x)=C(x)\).

Example 3

Fixed costs are \(\$1{,}500\), each unit costs \(\$20\) to make, and each sells for \(\$45\). Write \(C(x)\), \(R(x)\), and \(P(x)\), and find the break-even point.

Let \(x\) be the number of units.

\[ C(x)=20x+1500 \]
\[ R(x)=45x \]
\[ \begin{aligned} P(x) &= R(x)-C(x) \\ &= 45x-(20x+1500) \\ &= 25x-1500 \end{aligned} \]

Break-even: set \(R(x)=C(x)\).

\[ \begin{aligned} 45x &= 20x+1500 \\ 25x &= 1500 \\ x &= 60 \end{aligned} \]

At \(60\) units, the revenue and cost are both \(R(60)=45(60)=\$2{,}700\). The company breaks even at \(60\) units.
