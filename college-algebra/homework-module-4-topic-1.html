Problem 9

Evaluate the function without graphing it.

\[ f(x) = \begin{cases} x^2 - 1 & \text{if } x < -1 \\ 2x + 5 & \text{if } -1 \le x < 3 \\ 10 & \text{if } x \ge 3 \end{cases} \]

Show all of your work.

  1. \(f(-3)\)
  2. \(f(-1)\)
  3. \(f(0)\)
  4. \(f(3)\)
  5. \(f(7)\)

For each input, first decide which piece it belongs to.

  1. \(-3 \lt -1\), so use \(x^2-1\): \(\;f(-3) = (-3)^2-1 = 8\)
  2. \(-1 \le -1 \lt 3\), so use \(2x+5\): \(\;f(-1) = 2(-1)+5 = 3\)
  3. \(-1 \le 0 \lt 3\), so use \(2x+5\): \(\;f(0) = 2(0)+5 = 5\)
  4. \(3 \ge 3\), so use \(10\): \(\;f(3) = 10\)
  5. \(7 \ge 3\), so use \(10\): \(\;f(7) = 10\)

Problem 10

Find all intercepts. State each one as an ordered pair.

\[ h(x) = \log_2(x^2 - 2x) - 3 \]

Show all of your work.

\(y\)-intercept: \(h(0)=\log_2(0)-3\), and \(\log_2 0\) is undefined. There is no \(y\)-intercept.

\(x\)-intercepts: set \(h(x)=0\), write the log equation in exponential form, and solve.

\[ \begin{aligned} \log_2(x^2-2x)-3 &= 0 \\ \log_2(x^2-2x) &= 3 \\ x^2-2x &= 2^3 \\ x^2-2x-8 &= 0 \\ (x-4)(x+2) &= 0 \end{aligned} \]

So \(x=4\) or \(x=-2\). Check: both give \(x^2-2x=8\), which is positive, so both work.

\(x\)-intercepts: \((4,\,0)\) and \((-2,\,0)\). \(y\)-intercept: none.

Problem 11

Determine whether the function is even, odd, or neither.

\[ h(x) = x^5 - 3x^3 \]

Show all of your work.

Find \(h(-x)\) and compare it with \(h(x)\).

\[ \begin{aligned} h(-x) &= (-x)^5-3(-x)^3 \\ &= -x^5+3x^3 \\ &= -(x^5-3x^3) \\ &= -h(x) \end{aligned} \]

Since \(h(-x)=-h(x)\), the function is odd.

Problem 13

Find and simplify the difference quotient for \(f(x) = 4x - 3x^2\).

Show all of your work.

First find \(f(x+h)\).

\[ \begin{aligned} f(x+h) &= 4(x+h)-3(x+h)^2 \\ &= 4x+4h-3(x^2+2xh+h^2) \\ &= 4x+4h-3x^2-6xh-3h^2 \end{aligned} \]

Then build the difference quotient and simplify.

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h} &= \frac{(4x+4h-3x^2-6xh-3h^2)-(4x-3x^2)}{h} \\ &= \frac{4h-6xh-3h^2}{h} \\ &= \frac{h(4-6x-3h)}{h} \\ &= 4-6x-3h \end{aligned} \]

Problem 14

The number of bacteria in a culture follows the law of exponential growth. Initially there are \(500\) bacteria. After \(2\) hours there are \(1500\) bacteria.

Show all of your work.

  1. Find the value of \(k\). Round to four decimal places, and write the model \(A(t)\), where \(t\) is the number of hours.
  2. How many bacteria will there be after \(5\) hours? Round to the nearest whole number.
  3. How long will it take for there to be \(10{,}000\) bacteria? Round to two decimal places.

a. The model has the form \(A(t)=500e^{kt}\), since there are \(500\) bacteria at the start. Use \(A(2)=1500\) to find \(k\).

\[ \begin{aligned} 1500 &= 500e^{2k} \\ 3 &= e^{2k} \\ \ln 3 &= 2k \\ k &= \frac{\ln 3}{2}\approx 0.5493 \end{aligned} \]
\[ A(t)=500e^{0.5493t} \]

b.

\[ \begin{aligned} A(5) &= 500e^{0.5493(5)} \\ &\approx 7794 \end{aligned} \]

There will be about \(7794\) bacteria after \(5\) hours.

c. Set \(A(t)=10{,}000\) and solve for \(t\).

\[ \begin{aligned} 10{,}000 &= 500e^{0.5493t} \\ 20 &= e^{0.5493t} \\ \ln 20 &= 0.5493t \\ t &= \frac{\ln 20}{0.5493}\approx 5.45 \end{aligned} \]

It will take about \(5.45\) hours.

Problem 15

Write the expression as a sum and/or difference of logarithms. Express powers as factors.

\[ \log\left(\dfrac{x^2\sqrt{x - 3}}{(x + 5)^3}\right) \]

Show all of your work.

Use the quotient and product rules, write the square root as the power \(\tfrac12\), then use the power rule.

\[ \begin{aligned} &= \log x^2+\log (x-3)^{1/2}-\log(x+5)^3 \\ &= 2\log x+\tfrac12\log(x-3)-3\log(x+5) \end{aligned} \]

Problem 16

Solve the equation.

\[ \log_2 x + \log_2(x - 2) = \log_2 15 \]

Show all of your work.

Use the product rule to combine the left side. Then the arguments must be equal.

\[ \begin{aligned} \log_2\big(x(x-2)\big) &= \log_2 15 \\ x(x-2) &= 15 \\ x^2-2x-15 &= 0 \\ (x-5)(x+3) &= 0 \end{aligned} \]

So \(x=5\) or \(x=-3\). Check: \(x=-3\) gives \(\log_2(-3)\), which is undefined, so it is a false solution. \(x=5\) gives \(\log_2 5+\log_2 3=\log_2 15\). It checks.

The solution is \(x=5\).

Problem 17

Solve.

\[ 2x^4 = 50x^2 \]

Show all of your work.

Move everything to one side and factor. Do not divide by \(x^2\), or you lose the solution \(x=0\).

\[ \begin{aligned} 2x^4-50x^2 &= 0 \\ 2x^2(x^2-25) &= 0 \\ 2x^2(x-5)(x+5) &= 0 \end{aligned} \]

The solutions are \(x=0\), \(x=5\), and \(x=-5\).
