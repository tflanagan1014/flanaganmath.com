Lesson 5: The Disk Method (y-Axis)

Rotate the graph of \(x = f(y)\) over \([c, d]\) about the \(y\)-axis. Now the disks are horizontal.

  • Radius: \(f(y_k)\)
  • Height: \(\Delta y\)
  • Volume: \(\pi\left[f(y_k)\right]^2\Delta y\)

The Disk Method (about the y-axis)

\[ V = \int_c^d \pi\left[f(y)\right]^2 dy \]

The curve \(x = \sqrt{\cos y}\) over \(0 \le y \le \frac{\pi}{2}\) is rotated about the \(y\)-axis. Find the volume of the solid.

\[ \begin{aligned} V &= \int_0^{\pi/2} \pi\left(\sqrt{\cos y}\right)^2 dy \\ &= \pi\int_0^{\pi/2} \cos y\,dy \\ &= \pi\left[\sin y\right]_0^{\pi/2} \\ &= \pi \end{aligned} \]
