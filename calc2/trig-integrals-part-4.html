Lesson 15: Trigonometric Integrals, Part 4

Product-to-Sum Identities

\[ \begin{aligned} &\sin A\sin B = \tfrac{1}{2}\left[\cos(A - B) - \cos(A + B)\right] \\ &\sin A\cos B = \tfrac{1}{2}\left[\sin(A - B) + \sin(A + B)\right] \\ &\cos A\cos B = \tfrac{1}{2}\left[\cos(A - B) + \cos(A + B)\right] \end{aligned} \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle \int \sin(3x)\sin(2x)\,dx\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \int \sin(3x)\sin(2x)\,dx &= \frac{1}{2}\int \left(\cos x - \cos(5x)\right)dx \\ &= \frac{\sin x}{2} - \frac{\sin(5x)}{10} + C \end{aligned} \]
