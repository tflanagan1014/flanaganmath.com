Lesson 14: Trigonometric Integrals, Part 3

  • Power of tangent is odd: save \(\sec x\tan x\), change the remaining even power of tangent with \(\tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta - 1\), and let \(u = \sec x\).
  • Power of secant is even: save \(\sec^2 x\), change the remaining even power of secant with \(\sec^2\theta = 1 + \tan^2\theta\), and let \(u = \tan x\).

Evaluate \(\displaystyle \int \tan^3 x\sec^{17} x\,dx\).

\[ \int \tan^3 x\sec^{17} x\,dx = \int \left(\sec^2 x - 1\right)\sec^{16} x\,(\sec x\tan x)\,dx \]

Let \(u = \sec x\).

\[ \int \left(u^{18} - u^{16}\right)du = \frac{\sec^{19} x}{19} - \frac{\sec^{17} x}{17} + C \]

Evaluate \(\displaystyle \int \tan^{20} x\sec^4 x\,dx\).

\[ \int \tan^{20} x\sec^4 x\,dx = \int \tan^{20} x\left(1 + \tan^2 x\right)\sec^2 x\,dx \]

Let \(u = \tan x\).

\[ \int \left(u^{20} + u^{22}\right)du = \frac{\tan^{21} x}{21} + \frac{\tan^{23} x}{23} + C \]
