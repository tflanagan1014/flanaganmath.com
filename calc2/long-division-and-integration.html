Lesson 18: Long Division and Integration

When the degree of the numerator is at least the degree of the denominator, divide first.

Evaluate \(\displaystyle \int \frac{x^3}{x^2 + 9}\,dx\).

Long division gives \(\dfrac{x^3}{x^2 + 9} = x - \dfrac{9x}{x^2 + 9}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \int \frac{x^3}{x^2 + 9}\,dx &= \int \left(x - \frac{9x}{x^2 + 9}\right)dx \\ &= \frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{9}{2}\ln\left(x^2 + 9\right) + C \end{aligned} \]
