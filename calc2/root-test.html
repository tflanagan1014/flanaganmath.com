The Root Test

Let \(L = \displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \sqrt[n]{|a_n|}\).

  • If \(L < 1\), the series converges.
  • If \(L > 1\), the series diverges.
  • If \(L = 1\), the test is inconclusive.

It helps to know that \(\sqrt[n]{n} \to 1\) as \(n \to \infty\).

Apply the Root Test to \(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} k^2\left(\frac{7}{6}\right)^k\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{n \to \infty} \sqrt[n]{n^2\left(\frac{7}{6}\right)^n} &= \lim_{n \to \infty} \left(\sqrt[n]{n}\right)^2 \cdot \frac{7}{6} \\ &= \frac{7}{6} > 1 \end{aligned} \]

The series diverges.

Apply the Root Test to \(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{k^4}{2^k}\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{n \to \infty} \sqrt[n]{\frac{n^4}{2^n}} &= \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\left(\sqrt[n]{n}\right)^4}{2} \\ &= \frac{1}{2} < 1 \end{aligned} \]

The series converges.

Apply the Root Test to \(\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left(\frac{k}{k + 2}\right)^k\).

\[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{n}{n + 2} = 1 \]

The Root Test is inconclusive. The Divergence Test settles it: \(\left(\frac{n}{n + 2}\right)^n \to e^{-2} \neq 0\), so the series diverges.
