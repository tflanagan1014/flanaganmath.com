Volume of Revolution Formulas

In each formula, \(f \ge g\).

1. Disk Method (about the x-axis)

\[ V = \int_a^b \pi\left[f(x)\right]^2 dx \]

2. Disk Method (about the y-axis)

\[ V = \int_c^d \pi\left[f(y)\right]^2 dy \]

3. Washer Method (about the x-axis)

\[ V = \int_a^b \pi\left(\left[f(x)\right]^2 - \left[g(x)\right]^2\right)dx \]

4. Washer Method (about the y-axis)

\[ V = \int_c^d \pi\left(\left[f(y)\right]^2 - \left[g(y)\right]^2\right)dy \]

5. Shell Method (about the y-axis)

\[ V = \int_a^b 2\pi x\,f(x)\,dx \]

6. Shell Method (about the x-axis)

\[ V = \int_c^d 2\pi y\,f(y)\,dy \]
